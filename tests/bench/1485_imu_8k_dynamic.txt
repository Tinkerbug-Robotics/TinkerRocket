# #1485 — "8k Dynamic": the IMU logs at 7,680 Hz to deployment, then 960 Hz.
#
# Rig: a board whose FC reads the IMU from its FIFO (the mini; V9/V10 once
# benched), FC and OC both on the #1485 images (flash the OC first). S1 -> O:
# every line quoted here is OC-side. USB power is enough.
#
# What it proves, end to end: the OC accepts the 8k Dynamic setting (cmd 67,
# sentinel 1) and relays it; the FC captures ~7,300-7,600 samples/s (the
# part's own rate, a few percent off the 7680 Hz step) and sends them ten to a
# frame over the 88.2 kHz link; the OC unpacks every batch with none bad and
# keeps up while logging. The deployment step-down to 960 Hz does NOT happen
# in a sim flight: the sim drops straight from apogee into a steady descent,
# with no shock or fall for the detector to see. The log's own IMU rate is the
# final witness: download the flight and read its per-type rates (the .bin
# should show the part's rate, ~7,300-7,600 Hz, of 0xA2 records).
#
# Watch the OC console's "[I2S] imu batches" line through the flight: the
# part's rate at 8k, bad 0. The batch count runs above samples/10 (~900/s at a
# ~300 Hz flight loop) because the FC flushes a partial batch every loop pass.
# rx_ovf / ring_peak in the verbose stats say whether the parser and the NAND
# flush keep up.

note === clean start: power on, the FC starts streaming ===
# Attaching the console resets the OC. A command sent while it is still
# restoring the FC rail is lost, and the power-on below then finds the rail
# already on and prints no start-up line — so let the boot finish first.
sleep 10
send 8 00
disconnect
sleep 5
connect
mark
send 8 01
# Board-neutral: the mini's radio start line differs from the V9's modem one.
expect_serial 30 Peripherals initialized
expect_serial 30 \[I2S\] imu batches

note === select 8k Dynamic (cmd 67, rate_hz = 1, little-endian) ===
sleep 8
mark
send 67 0100
expect_serial 10 IMU logging rate: DYNAMIC \(7680 Hz boost\) -> FlightComputer
refute_serial_since IMU logging rate setting .* rejected
note === the FC applies it on its next poll; the batches roughly double ===
sleep 10
# 6,800-8,099/s: the 7680 Hz step runs a few percent off on each part (the
# mini's measures ~7,300, a V9's ~7,560); the FC's boot line "ISM6 FIFO
# capture: ... Hz on this part (trim N)" gives the exact figure.
expect_serial 20 \[I2S\] imu batches [0-9]+/s -> samples ((6[89]|7[0-9])[0-9][0-9]|80[0-9][0-9])/s, bad 0

note === sim flight: 883 g, 77 N for 1.5 s, 5 m/s descent (~546 m, lands in ~2 min) ===
sleep 10
send 5 00c05c4400009a420000c03f0000a040
sleep 10
mark
send 6
expect_tlm 40 fs.SIM_ACTIVE==1
expect_tlm 40 st==INFLIGHT
expect_tlm 40 fs.LOGGING_ACTIVE==1
note === boost and coast at 8k: every batch good ===
sleep 10
refute_serial_since imu batches .* bad [1-9]
expect_tlm 240 st==LANDED
refute_serial_since imu batches .* bad [1-9]
expect_serial_since finalizeFlight OK

note === back to the default, 4k Dynamic (sentinel 0) ===
sleep 20
mark
send 67 0000
expect_serial 10 IMU logging rate: DYNAMIC \(3840 Hz boost\) -> FlightComputer
