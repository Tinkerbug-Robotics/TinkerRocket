# timeline, and is the BS actually hearing the rocket over LoRa?

# initPeripherals() never ran and LoRa was never initialised — the BS then hears
# nothing and every uplink test fails for a reason that has nothing to do with
# the fix under test. Fix with a rail-up first:
#   bench_ble_cmd.py --name "RC V9" --cmd 8 --payload 01
#
# The BS advertises as `nimble`, NOT as its configured name, so connect to it by
# address (bench_ble_cmd.py --scan to find it) rather than by --name.
