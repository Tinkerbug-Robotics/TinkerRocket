# #1484 — on the mini, starting the radio must not take the log flash's SPI pins.
#
# Rig: rocket-computer-mini, S1 -> O (every line quoted here is OC-side). USB
# power is enough; no battery, no supercap needed. Start in any rail state: the
# first step restarts the OC. Needs a chip with NO flights on it (a fresh board),
# or change the two counts below.
#
# Why this shape. The NAND's begin() and its recovery scan run BEFORE the radio
# starts, so a power-on alone looks healthy even with the bug. The damage is to
# everything after the radio start: the sim flight's pages and its index entry.
# So: power on, fly, then restart the OC and let the next begin() read back what
# the chip actually holds, before the radio can take the pins again.
#
# With the bug as filed: three "GPIO nn is conflict with others" lines at the
# radio start, 40-odd "bad block N mid-flight" lines during the flight, and
# after the restart the index still reads 0 flight(s) (bench 2026-09-24).
# With the fix: none of that, and the index holds the flight.
#
# Reconnect quickly after each restart. Once the OC's 10 s USB grace ends and
# light sleep unblocks, its console can go silent and miss the begin() line
# below. The flight list over BLE is the independent witness:
#   tools/ble_flight_download.py list   (set TARGET_NAME to this board's name)

note === clean start: a deliberate power-off restarts the OC with the rail off ===
send 8 00
disconnect
sleep 5
connect

note === power on: the NAND comes up, then the radio ===
mark
send 8 01
expect_serial 30 FLIGHTLOG: up: 0 flight\(s\) in index
expect_serial 30 LORA: LoRa radio initialized
refute_serial_since is conflict with others and be overwritten
expect_tlm 30 h.STORAGE==OK

note === sim flight: 883 g, 77 N for 1.5 s, 5 m/s descent (about 546 m, lands in ~2 min) ===
sleep 10
send 5 00c05c4400009a420000c03f0000a040
sleep 10
send 6
expect_tlm 40 fs.SIM_ACTIVE==1
expect_tlm 40 st==INFLIGHT
expect_tlm 40 fs.LOGGING_ACTIVE==1
expect_tlm 240 st==LANDED
refute_serial_since mid-flight: relocating
note === let the flight close, so the index write is due ===
# Searched from the power-on mark: it can print right at LANDED.
expect_serial 120 finalizeFlight OK
# Search from here: frames from before the flight also read LOGGING_ACTIVE==0.
mark
expect_tlm 120 fs.LOGGING_ACTIVE==0
sleep 20

note === restart the OC; begin() re-reads the index before the radio starts ===
send 8 00
disconnect
sleep 5
connect
mark
send 8 01
expect_serial 30 FLIGHTLOG: up: 1 flight\(s\) in index
refute_serial_since begin failed
refute_serial_since is conflict with others and be overwritten
note === done: download the flight separately and check its bytes ===
