# #1211 / #1228 — a flight logger that failed to come up is retried on the
# ground, and only the logging half of initPeripherals() is re-entered.
#
# Rig: V9 pair, S1 -> O (every line quoted here is OC-side). Rail OFF at the
# start: the cmd-8 power-on is the entry point. No GNSS fix needed.
#
# IMAGE. This needs the bench fault image — the V9's NAND sits on the always-on
# +3V3 rail and cannot be made to fail for real:
#
#   cd tinkerrocket-idf/projects/out_computer
#   idf.py -B build_bench -DTR_BOARD_V9=1 -DTR_OC_BENCH_DEBUG=1 \
#          -DTR_OC_BENCH_FAIL_LOGGER_BEGIN=2 build flash
#
# The first TWO logger.begin() calls are reported as failed without being
# made: the power-on's, then retry 1 at +10 s. Retry 2 is due 30 s after that
# (+40 s from power-on); a sim flight is started so that it straddles the due
# time, which holds the retry — without spending it — first for INFLIGHT and
# then for LANDED. cmd 7 takes the FC out of LANDED, the hold lifts, and retry
# 2 runs a REAL begin(): the board's first, so this is also the ordinary V9
# bring-up landing a few seconds later than usual. Never fly a -bench image.
#
# What each block proves (the issue's three "done when" lines):
#   power-on failure     the schedule is armed; LoRa, I2S and I2C still came up
#   retry 1              initPeripherals() re-entered ONLY the logging half —
#                        no second "Initializing peripherals", no second I2S
#                        or LoRa bring-up line
#   deferred (flight)    the INFLIGHT hold, attempt not spent
#   deferred (LANDED)    the #317 hold, same
#   retry 2 RECOVERED    a real begin() on the retry; h.STORAGE goes BAD -> OK
#                        without a reboot
#
# What this cannot prove: TR_LogToFlash's own re-entrancy after a GENUINE
# failure (ring and mutexes already allocated). The fault hook skips the call,
# so that half lives in tests_cpp/test_log_to_flash_begin_retry.cpp.

note === rail on: the first begin() is faulted, everything else comes up ===
mark
send 8 01
expect_serial 20 BENCH FAULT \(TR_OC_BENCH_FAIL_LOGGER_BEGIN\).*\(1 more to go\)
expect_serial 10 TR_LogToFlash begin failed .* Retry 1 of 3 in 10 s
expect_serial 30 LoRa NVS:
expect_serial 30 I2S slave RX ready
expect_serial 10 Peripherals initialized\.

note === retry 1 at +10 s: faulted again; the radio/link half is NOT re-run ===
# The origin is set here, a few seconds after power-on, so the +10 s retry
# cannot land before it (the harness searches forward from the last mark).
mark
expect_serial 25 1228: retrying the flight logger \(retry 1 of 3\)
expect_serial 5 BENCH FAULT .*\(0 more to go\)
expect_serial 5 1228: logger retry 1 failed
expect_serial_since Retry 2 of 3 in 30 s
refute_serial_since Initializing peripherals\.\.\.
refute_serial_since I2S slave RX ready
refute_serial_since LoRa NVS:
expect_tlm 30 h.STORAGE==BAD
health

note === a sim flight straddles retry 2's due time (+40 s): deferred, not spent ===
# 300 g / 11.7 N / 2 s, apogee ~232 m — the 1148 profile; lands on its own.
send 5 0000964333333b41000000400000a040
sleep 4
send 6
expect_tlm 30 st==INFLIGHT
mark
expect_serial 40 1228: logger retry due but deferred: flight in progress
refute_serial_since 1228: retrying the flight logger

note === landed: still deferred, now for the 317 lockout reason ===
wait_tlm 180 st==LANDED
expect_serial 15 1228: logger retry due but deferred: LANDED
refute_serial_since 1228: retrying the flight logger
expect_tlm 20 h.STORAGE==BAD

note === cmd 7 takes the FC out of LANDED: the hold lifts, retry 2 runs for real ===
mark
send 7
expect_tlm 30 st!=LANDED
expect_serial 15 1228: retrying the flight logger \(retry 2 of 3\)
expect_serial 60 1228: flight logger RECOVERED on retry 2 after
expect_serial_since FLIGHTLOG.*up: [0-9]+ flight\(s\) in index
expect_tlm 30 h.STORAGE!=BAD
health
refute_serial_since Initializing peripherals\.\.\.
refute_serial_since I2S slave RX ready
refute_serial_since LoRa NVS:
note === done ===
