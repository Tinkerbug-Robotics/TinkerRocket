# #1484 follow-up — the mini's radio joins the NAND's SPI bus without an error line.
#
# Rig: rocket-computer-mini, S1 -> O (every line quoted here is OC-side). USB
# power is enough. Any number of flights on the chip.
#
# Since #1488 every power-on printed two of
#   E (...) spi_common: spi_bus_initialize(895): SPI bus already initialized.
# 151 ms apart, right before "LoRa radio initialized": EspHal::spiBegin() ran
# once per LLCC68::begin() attempt, and the E220 needs two (it answers to
# "SX1261", not "LLCC68"). With the fix: no such line, and the radio still
# comes up on the bus the NAND owns.

note === clean start: a deliberate power-off restarts the OC with the rail off ===
send 8 00
disconnect
sleep 5
connect

note === power on: the NAND comes up, then the radio joins its bus ===
mark
send 8 01
expect_serial 30 FLIGHTLOG: up: \d+ flight\(s\) in index
expect_serial 30 LORA: LoRa radio initialized
refute_serial_since SPI bus already initialized
refute_serial_since EspHal: SPI (bus init|add device) failed
refute_serial_since LoRa init failed
refute_serial_since is conflict with others and be overwritten
expect_tlm 30 h.STORAGE==OK

note === second power cycle: the same again ===
send 8 00
disconnect
sleep 5
connect
mark
send 8 01
expect_serial 30 LORA: LoRa radio initialized
refute_serial_since SPI bus already initialized
refute_serial_since EspHal: SPI (bus init|add device) failed
expect_tlm 30 h.STORAGE==OK
