# #1231 phase 3 — S1 -> O (OUT COMPUTER console), both boards on the new firmware, OC on
# the "-bench-cfgdrop" image.  The OC's own view: the divergence line when its cache and
# the FC's copy part ways (at rail-on, and on a dropped frame), the "matches again" line
# when they meet, and the dropped-frame case end to end.
#
# Timing rules learned on 2026-09-11: (1) never arm the hook while a previous command
# may still be inside its 3-delivery window — a 3 s settle first; (2) lines that land
# within milliseconds of each other are checked from history (mark + _since) after one
# forward wait, never as a chain of forward waits.
note === 3p: create a divergence with the hook — the OC cache moves to D, the FC keeps what it has ===
wait_tlm 25 st!=NONE
send 8 01
sleep 12
wait_tlm 20 fs.PWR_PIN_ON==1
sleep 3
mark
send 200 03ce
sleep 1
send 34 01000000404000000000000001010000c843000000000000
expect_serial 20 \[TEST\] config frame type=0xCE DROPPED at pack time \(cfgdrop hook, delivery 3 of 3 armed\)
sleep 1
expect_serial_since \[TEST\] config frame type=0xCE DROPPED at pack time \(cfgdrop hook, delivery 1 of 3 armed\)
expect_serial_since Cmd 0xCD cleared after 3 deliveries
expect_serial_since \[TEST\] cfgdrop hook disarmed
sleep 6
expect_serial_since FC deployment config differs from OC cache — FC\(nvs\)=\[0/0/0\.0 1/1/250\.0 0/0/0\.0 1/0/5\.0\] OC=\[1/0/3\.0 0/0/0\.0 1/1/400\.0 0/0/0\.0\]
refute_serial_since Queued pyro config readback
note === 3a: rail cycle — the OC comes up fresh; the FC (E) reports, the cache (D) differs; the first report must say so ===
send 8 00
sleep 10
disconnect
sleep 2
connect

mark
send 8 01
sleep 14

mark
send 20
expect_serial 15 Queued pyro config readback \(\d+ bytes, from FC report\)

expect_tlm 15 src==fc
expect_tlm 15 fnv==true
expect_tlm 15 p1e==false
expect_tlm 15 p2v==250.0
expect_tlm 15 p4e==true
