# gvlb258-patch-test — GVLB258.A patch test board

The Tinker-Beetle V2's GNSS antenna, a Taoglas GVLB258.A single-feed L1/L5 stacked patch, on a board as wide as the
Beetle, with an SMA jack on the back. It feeds the PX100 carrier's SMA input
([`gnss-px100-18mm-hv/`](../gnss-px100-18mm-hv/) J1), so the carrier can run the Beetle's antenna and RF chain on the
bench, on the rig's sky tests and, if it earns a seat, on a test flight. Started 2026-11-27.

**State:** V1 drawn 2026-11-27. ERC clean; DRC with schematic parity: no errors, no warnings, nothing unconnected.
Not reviewed by the owner, not committed, not ordered.

## Feed

`RF_FEED` runs straight down the back from the patch pin to the jack's center pin, 15.0 mm, as a grounded coplanar
line: 1.0 mm wide with 0.25 mm gaps to the back ground pour, over the front ground pour 1.53 mm away. For JLC's
2-layer 1.6 mm FR-4 (er about 4.5) that is 49 Ω by the Wadell grounded-coplanar formula with 35 µm copper. Confirm
it in JLC's impedance calculator before ordering; 45 or 55 Ω would still be a return loss of about 25 dB. Ground vias (0.4/0.3 mm) run on both sides of the line at 1.5 mm pitch, 1.25 mm from its
center, and in a 3.6 mm grid over the rest of the board.

The board carries no matching or DC-block parts on purpose. The carrier V3 already has the Beetle's whole feed
network after its J1: `C81` (shunt pad, at the feed) → `D6` (ESD) → `C20` (series, the Beetle's `C7`) → `C82` (shunt
pad) → the diplexer. With this board on J1 and **JP1 open (no DC on J1)**, the carrier sees the Beetle's chain from the
patch pin on. The difference is the 15 mm line plus the SMA pair and any cable in between. Tune with `C81`/`C82` on the
carrier, as on the Beetle. The back silkscreen says "PX100 CARRIER: JP1 OPEN".

Patch land and keep-outs are the library footprint's (`Footprints:ANT_GVLB258.A_TAOGLAS`, the Beetle's): 1.0 mm
plated hole, no copper within 3.0 mm diameter on the front, 3.5 mm on the back. The jack's center pad has 0.5 mm of
pour clearance on both sides.

## How it compares with the Beetle

- **Ground plane:** 26.83 × 35.5 mm here against the Beetle's 26.83 × 70.35 mm. Taoglas measures the GVLB258.A on
  70 × 70 mm. The lower patch (L5) is the one that suffers most on a small ground. This board is the width-limited
  case, shorter than the Beetle. So its L5 is, if anything, a pessimistic stand-in. A longer copy is a one-line change
  (`L` in `gen/make_pcb.py`, plus moving `SMA` and `HOLES`).
- **What is under the patch:** here, only ground. On the Beetle, the LoRa module and the GNSS front end sit on the back
  under the patch, and the LoRa antenna is about 15 mm away.
- **Mounting:** two holes, both at the SMA end; the patch end is free. For a flight, back the patch end with foam or
  tape, or ask for a second pair of holes (about +4.7 mm of length).

The TinkerNav board's own footprint and STEP for this jack came from SnapMagic, whose license forbids redistributing
them. This repo is public, so the footprint and model here are redrawn rather than copied.

Rerunning `make_sch.py` keeps the symbol UUIDs (they come from the references). Rerunning `make_pcb.py` rebuilds the
board from nothing, so it overwrites hand edits.
