# gvlb258-patch-test — fabrication and assembly notes

**V1**, drawn 2026-11-27. No gerbers yet.

```
TINKERROCKET GVLB258.A PATCH TEST BOARD V1 - FABRICATION AND ASSEMBLY NOTES
BOARD 26.83 x 35.5 mm, 1.0 mm CORNER RADIUS, 2 LAYER, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz.

1. STACKUP: JLC 2-LAYER 1.6 mm FR-4 (DIELECTRIC ABOUT 1.53 mm, er ABOUT 4.5).
   RF_FEED (B.Cu, 1.0 mm WIDE, 0.25 mm GAPS TO THE B.Cu POUR) IS SIZED
   FOR 50 OHM GROUNDED COPLANAR ON THAT BOARD. NO IMPEDANCE CONTROL
   ORDERED; CHECK THE WIDTH IN JLC'S CALCULATOR BEFORE ORDERING.
2. VIAS: 0.4 mm / 0.3 mm DRILL ONLY, TENTED BOTH SIDES. NONE IN PADS.
   THE PATCH SITS ON TENTED VIAS IN THE F.Cu GROUND.
3. FINISH: ANY (HASL IS FINE: EVERY JOINT IS HAND-SOLDERED).
   SOLDER MASK BOTH SIDES; SILKSCREEN ON THE BACK ONLY (BOARD TEXT).
4. NO SMD PARTS, NO STENCIL, NO OVEN. ASSEMBLY IS BY HAND:
   a. J1 (RFPC-SMA28-F) FIRST: INSERT FROM THE BACK, BODY ON THE BACK,
      SOLDER THE FOUR POSTS AND THE CENTER PIN ON THE FRONT. THE POST
      JOINTS ARE 1.0 mm FROM WHERE THE PATCH CERAMIC WILL SIT: KEEP
      THEM LOW AND CLEAN SO THE PATCH STILL SEATS FLAT.
   b. AE1 (GVLB258.A): REMOVE THE ADHESIVE LINER, PUT THE PIN THROUGH
      THE 1.0 mm HOLE FROM THE FRONT, PRESS THE PATCH FLAT, ALIGNED TO
      THE SILK OUTLINE, AND SOLDER THE PIN ON THE BACK TO THE 2.2 mm
      PAD WHERE RF_FEED STARTS. NOTHING ELSE ON THE BACK WITHIN 1.75 mm
      OF THE PIN.
5. CHECK: CONTINUITY FROM J1 CENTER TO THE AE1 PIN JOINT. EXPECT NONE
   FROM J1 CENTER TO J1 SHELL; IF IT READS SHORT, LOOK FOR A BRIDGE AT
   THE PIN OR THE J1 CENTER JOINT FIRST.
6. USE: PX100 CARRIER (gnss-px100-18mm-hv) WITH JP1 OPEN, SO NO DC
   REACHES THE PATCH. A SHORT SMA MALE-TO-MALE CABLE OR A STRAIGHT
   ADAPTER BETWEEN THE TWO JACKS.
```
