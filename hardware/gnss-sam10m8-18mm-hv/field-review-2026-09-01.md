# SAM-M10Q GNSS carrier — field review: why the buck carrier used 7–10 satellites where the LDO carrier used 27

**Board under review:** `hardware/gnss-sam10m8-18mm-hv` as fabricated — tag `gnss-sam10m8-18mm-hv-v2.1.0` (commit 5e3a426, gerbers plotted 2026-08-09), TPS62913 buck, first article with U1 reworked (#906)
**Control:** `SAM10M8_18mm_HighVoltage` as fabricated 2026-06-10 (archive backup zip `…-2026-06-10_061200.zip`; the archive's top-level Jul-3 files were edited after fab and disagree with each other), ADP7142 LDO
**Date:** 2026-09-01/02
**Trigger:** side-by-side field result of ~27 vs ~11 satellites. The comparison turned out to be the BARC launch of 2026-08-29: the V8 av-bay computer (old carrier) vs the V9 nosecone computer (new carrier) in the same rocket.
**Method:** seven independent review lenses (EMI, power integrity, antenna environment, configuration/firmware, assembly, flight data, test design) over the as-fabricated board files, netlists, firmware and the flight and base-station binaries; 61 raw findings merged to 12; each merged finding put to two adversarial verifiers (physics/datasheets and evidence/alternatives). Numbers below are the verified ones; where a verifier corrected a finding, the corrected value is used and the original is named. Scripts and per-agent notes are in the session scratchpad, not the repo; every number is reproducible from the files cited in section 7.

## 1. Verdict

**The deficit is real, large and stable, and it lives on the V9 nose installation as a whole, not demonstrably on the carrier design.** On 2026-08-29 the new carrier's installation (repo board, module FB80A88FA854, U1 reworked, V9 host, nosecone with its own pack) used 9–10 satellites at the instant the old LDO carrier (module B9A8090FB454, V8 host, av-bay) used 26–27, and held a pad median of 8 (0–11, pdop 2.0) against 26 (20–30, pdop 1.0) over the same 17.8 minutes, same sky, same firmware image, both modules at the default clock. Keeping one third of the visible satellites means only those above roughly 40° elevation survived, which for a small-ground-plane patch is a 10–16 dB uniform C/N0 loss (8 dB is the firm floor; a 6 dB loss would still leave two thirds of the sky usable).

**No design-level mechanism on the new carrier reaches that magnitude.** The ground plane is measurably better than the old board's (both inner layers solid GND, 21 vs 12 GND vias under the module), configuration and OTP state are identical, the buck stage is a faithful TI reference implementation, and low-frequency supply quality is equivalent to the LDO's within microvolts. The one genuine design difference that can hurt GNSS — the 2.2 MHz forced-PWM buck with spread spectrum off, whose harmonic comb always has a line inside every L1 passband, switching 1.4 mm from the module axis on the far side of the board — is real and always on, but two independent estimates put its radiated coupling into the patch at 1–3 dB central (6–12 dB upper tail), and every conducted supply path is at least 20 dB, more likely 45–70 dB, short. No flown buck-family comparator exists: the other healthy receivers that day were on-board GNSS on V6 computers, not carriers (owner's correction, 2026-09-02), and the archive `SAM10M8_18mm_HV_V2` buck board has, as far as the record shows, never flown. The buck's share therefore rests on the two estimates alone and is unmeasured; steps 5 and 6 of the test sequence measure it directly.

**The two candidates that can produce the observed magnitude are both unit- and installation-level, and both are unmeasured:** (1) something conductive or ferrous in the nose sled within a few centimetres of the patch — #847 records tens of µT of hard iron in that mount, the airframe model carries ~0.55 kg of unmodelled nose contents, and on the pad the patch looks straight up through whatever sits at the tip; (2) front-end damage to the reworked U1 — the rework method, excursion count and bake are unrecorded and the module was past its MSL-4 floor life. A 45-minute side-by-side with both carriers out of the airframes, logging per-satellite C/N0, separates carrier from installation; a carrier swap between hosts then separates carrier from unit. The buck remains a plausible few-dB contributor to be measured with UBX-MON-SPAN, not the primary suspect.

**Flight-safety corollary that stands regardless of cause:** the V9 had no usable GNSS solution from T+0.5 to T+9.5 s (position lag growing to 451 m, then 11 epochs with no satellites used and 27 epochs of 3-satellite 2D fixes); the same nav-filter stall at boost appears on the healthy V8, but only the weak receiver failed to recover before T+9.5 s.

### 2.2 What the data cannot distinguish

1. Carrier vs installation. Every measurement of the new board (pad, #847 bench on 08-24, flight, post-landing) was taken inside the same nose sled. The #847 "11 sats" reading was in the final mount with the rocket lying flat; it shows the deficit is static, not that it survives outside the mount. No out-of-mount sky-view count of the new board exists (the 08-27 capture was indoors, 5 sats).
2. Carrier vs host. V9 (P4/S3, with TPS62152, TLV62569, TPS61094 and three VLS3012CX-2R2M-1 inductors per rc_netlist.net) vs V8. Every fixed-frequency switcher on either host also has a harmonic within +-fSW/2 of L1. "The old board has no switching source" is true of the carrier, not of either installation.
3. Design vs unit. The compared new board is a single first article whose U1 was reworked wholesale (#906) by a method nobody recorded.
4. Broadband vs spur. numSV cannot distinguish a uniform front-end/antenna loss from an in-band comb line raising the effective noise floor (CFG-ITFM is off by default) or from a pattern/shadowing change. Per-satellite C/N0 was never logged.
5. Slow creep is not diagnostic. Every receiver in the logs creeps 10-20 min (old carrier 21 -> 29 over 15 min); used-satellite count grows only as ephemerides decode (~27 dB-Hz needed), so any C/N0 loss of any origin produces the same shape. The V9 differs in level, not shape.

## 3. Ranked causes

Likelihoods are the verifiers' revised values. "Can explain" asks whether the mechanism can reach the 10-16 dB (>= 8 dB) magnitude on its own.

### 3.1 Installation: nose sled contents, orientation, cone (likelihood medium, can explain: yes, unbounded)

Mechanism. The patch gives +3 dBic at boresight, ~0 dBic at 60 deg, -3 to -4 dBic at 90 deg, back lobe -5/-9 dBic, two narrow -15 to -18 dBic nulls (IM Fig. 20, 50 x 50 mm plane). Orientation alone therefore redistributes satellites by a few and costs <= 3 dB over the upper 120 deg cone. A conductor smaller than ~lambda/2 (95 mm) within 5-20 mm of the patch acts as a near-field reflector/detuner (several dB, orientation dependent); a conductor spanning the ~100 mm cone, a foil-pouch pack across it, or metallic-pigment paint approaches a shadow and can reach 6-10 dB and beyond. IM 4.3.1.1: parts taller than 3 mm at least 10 mm away, any cover at least 5 mm away.

Evidence. examples/flights/L2_100mm_Rolly_Polly_V/rocket/100_mm_Fiberglass_Steerable.ork: nosecone G10 fiberglass, wall 1.549 mm, 409 mm ogive. Computed slab loss at 1575 MHz (er 4.7, tan d 0.015): 0.05 dB normal, 0.18 dB at 60 deg, 0.57-1.86 dB even for 6 mm of glass-epoxy: the cone itself is negligible unless painted with metallic pigment. The .ork's 0.8 kg mass override vs a computed 246 g bare shell implies ~0.55 kg of contents (sled, V9, pack, ballast) whose position relative to the patch is unrecorded. GitHub #847 (2026-08-24): the in-mount magnetometer calibration applied out of the mount reads |B| = 74 uT against a fitted 46.5 uT, i.e. tens of uT of hard iron in the sled, the class of body IM 4.3.1.1 warns about. Post-landing: 9/9/9/10/10/9 sats at 4-6 m displacement, 10-13 after the 76 m carry, 12-16 after the 110 m move; RSSI -85 -> -52 dBm at 4-6 min tracks the operator walking to the landing site (base-station phone_fix events at t = 1346-1399 s), not the rocket.

Verifier corrections applied. The finding's central claim that the data bound installation to 3-5 of the 17 satellites is withdrawn: whole-sled re-orientation moves the count 2-3 per move, but everything inside the sled moved with it, so the sled's internals are untested. Argument (a) (the 08-24 in-mount reading) says nothing about the mount's share. Argument (c) (slow creep) does not discriminate board from mount. "No document records the cone material" is wrong (the .ork does); paint and contents are what is unrecorded.

Discriminating test. Section 4 step 1, then step 3.

### 3.2 Unit: the reworked U1's RF front end (likelihood low, can explain: partly, only via a damaged SAW/LNA/patch)

Mechanism. IM 5.3 rates the module for one production reflow plus one rework reflow, peak 245 C, TAL 40-60 s above 217 C, and states that a hot air gun "can lead to overheating and severely damage the module"; MSL 4 (72 h floor life, IM Table 22 / DS Table 20); ESD into the LNA via the ~10 pF patch (IM 5.1.1). SAW insertion loss, LNA gain loss, a cracked or delaminated patch, or a re-melted feed each cost dB on every satellite regardless of supply, leaving a receiver that still configures, tracks and fixes.

Evidence. #906: board dead on arrival (no UART on pad 13 GNSS_TX, PPS flashing, power LED on), "now works after reworking U1"; correction comment: "rework was wholesale, no joint was probed first". #717 comment 2026-08-24: module FB80A88FA854 on the first article. Fab 2026-08-09, rework before 2026-08-24: floor life certainly exceeded, no bake recorded, because nothing about the rework was recorded.

Verifier corrections applied. No source says hot air, removal, or re-attachment; the record supports only "wholesale rework of U1, method unrecorded". Excursion count is 2-4 (an in-place reflow would be exactly the one rework cycle IM 5.3 permits). Two legs cannot reach the deficit and are struck as causes: open GND joints (one surviving 1.8 x 1.5 mm LGA joint is <= 1 ohm at 1575 MHz and the module ground couples to the pour through ~15-20 pF regardless, < 1 dB; both carriers are already below u-blox's 40 x 40 mm plane threshold and the old one tracks 27 there) and F.Cu collateral (19 parts inside the courtyard, not 33; C5/C6 sit at x = 62.9, outside it; 100 pF is 723 ohm at 2.2 MHz). The #847 "11 sats" reading is not a bench baseline (rocket flat, orientation and dwell unrecorded). The LANDED climb to 16-18 shows a several-dB deficit, not a fixed ceiling. No positive sign of damage exists (460800 baud, SPG 5.10, 18.18 Hz, 3D fixes with h_acc 1 m, PPS alive).

Discriminating test. Section 4 steps 2, 4, 5 cell (i), 7. The fabricated board was hand-assembled; do not apply heat before steps 4 and 5 are done.

### 3.3 Buck harmonic comb radiated into the patch (likelihood low, can explain: partly; guaranteed present, central 1-3 dB)

Mechanism. U3 TPS62913 with S-CONF = VIN (netlist: U3.1 EN/SYNC, U3.6 VIN, U3.10 S-CONF one net) runs 2.2 MHz, spread spectrum off, forced PWM to no load (TI Table 7-1, 7.4.1). At the 12-16 mA module load the inductor current swings 330/378/415 mA pk-pk at 6.4/7.4/8.4 V and reverses every cycle (-0.17..+0.20 A). Harmonics n = 709-731 form ~23 lines across 1559-1610 MHz: n = 716 = 1575.20 MHz, n = 727-729 inside GLONASS L1OF; over the 1.98-2.42 MHz tolerance a line is always within +-1.21 MHz of every carrier (at 2.42 MHz n = 651 lands on 1575.42 exactly), so the pre-fab "0.2 MHz off L1" remark is incidental and the comb is generic to any fixed-frequency buck without spread spectrum. Emitter: ~5 mm2 of SW copper (1.7 mm2 zone x51.75-54.10 / y43.77-44.58, L8 pad 1, U3 pad 2) plus L8's SW-side turns at F.Cu (54.05, 43.03), 1.4 mm laterally from the module axis on the far side of the 1.59 mm board, behind two solid GND inner layers (549.1 mm2 each; only the GNSS_TX/RX vias break them within 4 mm of L8).

Magnitude (both verifiers, independent). Edges are current-slewed by I_L into the SW-node capacitance during dead time (0.75-4 V/ns for 50-200 pF, i.e. 2-10 ns for 7.4 V) unless the dead time is shorter; sub-ns hard edges are the upper bound. Harmonic amplitude at n = 716: 0.05-1 mV (central ~0.2 mV). Total power radiated into the F-side half-space at 1575.2 MHz: -113 dBm (zone only), -104 dBm (5 mm2, 0.97 mV), -95 dBm only if L8's winding is counted at ~15 mm2 equivalent with sub-ns edges. Realistic capture from behind two planes into a patch facing away: -1..-14 dB, giving -96..-127 dBm at the antenna port against -104 dBm (6 dB C/N0 loss) / -99 dBm (10 dB) needed vs kTB + NF = -109 dBm in 2.046 MHz. Central case 1-3 dB, upper tail 6-12 dB; even the upper bound cannot supply the > 10 dB the count implies. A free-running RC oscillator's cycle jitter multiplies by n: 20-100 ps gives a 27-690 kHz wide line at n = 716, so these are jitter-broadened partial-band noise, not CW, and the M10's cwSuppression is not a reliable canceller.

Verifier corrections applied. "Both edges hard-switch" -> current-slewed, upper bound only. "45-60 dB isolation required vs 50-70 dB expected, straddles the threshold" -> replaced by the absolute bookkeeping above (the -75..-60 dBV node voltage must not be converted to dBm in 50 ohm). "A CW line degrades every satellite equally, matching the uniform deficit" -> deleted; no per-satellite C/N0 was logged and CW jamming of C/A is PRN/Doppler selective. "+-1.1 MHz" -> +-1.21 MHz. Test (5) S-CONF blade cut is contested: the physics verifier finds the 0.2 mm stub (51.55, 44.74)-(51.55, 45.23) under U3's 2 x 2 mm body (F.Fab x49.815-51.815, y43.32-45.32) with only the pad tip exposed; treat it as requiring U3 lifted or a respin footprint. The 1 MHz cell is a weak discriminator anyway (two lines inside +-1.023 MHz, integrated in-band power down only ~3.4 dB, and 2.2 uH at D = 0.52 violates TI 8.2.2.2.2). Likelihood medium -> low, can_explain yes -> partly (both verifiers).

Discriminating test. Section 4 steps 5 and 6.

### 3.4 Conducted GHz hash on +3V3 into VCC / V_IO (likelihood low, can explain: no)

Mechanism as filed: SW harmonics through L8's interwinding capacitance to VO, through FB1 to +3V3, into VCC pin 17 with every capacitor above SRF.

Verifier corrections applied (both). At 1.575 GHz every MLCC is mounting-inductance limited regardless of value (0805 ~12 ohm, 0402 ~8-9 ohm), so "only C6 100 pF shunts" and "V_IO has no HF decoupling" are false (C16 100 nF is 3.25 mm from pad 2, the same class as C6's 3.83 mm from pad 17; the nearest VCC caps are C14/C12 through the via at (61.25, 40.13)). FB1 782853200 is ~25-33 ohm and resistive/capacitive past its 700 MHz peak (datasheet |Z| flat 32-35 ohm from 600 MHz to 3 GHz), not "a few ohms". V_IO feeds digital IOs, clock and backup (IM 4.1.2), not the RF domain; VCC feeds the RF domain through an internal bead and local decoupling (MAX-M10S IM UBX-20053088 3.1.1, same UBX-M10050 chip; the SAM-M10Q typical design shows no external VCC caps). Chain attenuation SW -> VO -> +3V3 -> module: -44..-52 dB (evidence lens) to ~-60 dB (physics lens), giving 0.1-6.5 uV at the pad against 110 uV-1.9 mV required for 20-40 dB of internal isolation: 20-70 dB short. The 37 mm B.Cu +3V3 route is not a mechanism: only 4.9 mm of 0.127 mm segments lie inside the 15.5 mm body, they are the FB-sense branch and the C16 stub (V_IO is fed by the 0.4 mm perimeter route), the route lies between two solid GND planes, and the OLD board tracked 27 sats with a 461 mm2 +3V3 plane 0.1 mm under the module. Likelihood medium -> low, can_explain partly -> no (physics) / minor contributor at most (evidence).

Test. None needed for the deficit; the FB1-lift experiment (step 5) covers it as a by-product. The proposed SMA-pigtail spectrum-analyser check would read 0.1-1 uV (-127..-107 dBm) below a typical SA floor and picks up radiated hash as well; drop it.

### 3.5 First-stage COUT 40-80 uF window (likelihood low; ruled out as a cause; still an open verification item)

TI 8.2.2.2.3 requires 40-80 uF effective first-stage capacitance with a second L-C stage. 3 x 22 uF Samsung CL21A226MOQNNNE (16 V X5R 0805) at 3.33 V: no part-specific bias curve is obtainable (the DigiKey-hosted sheet is the 0.85 mm CL21A226MOCLRNC variant and has only a generic 10 uF/6.3 V example; Samsung's pages say "Graphs for the item are not supported"; Samsung lists the part NRND, replacement CL21A226MOYNNN). A same-class proxy (Murata GRM21BR61C226ME44) reads -31 % at 3.3 V: ~45 uF nominal (in window), ~36 uF at -20 % tolerance (below the floor). TI's own reference design (Table 8-1/8-2, 3 x 22 uF 10 V X7S) carries the identical stack, so the design is no more marginal than TI's validated circuit unless the Samsung 16 V X5R derates worse than TDK's 10 V X7S; that comparison is the open question. The 126 kHz second-stage pole is inside TI's 56-356 kHz compensated envelope, not a marginality indicator.

Why it cannot be the cause: even a 100 mV pp oscillation at 50-500 kHz on VCC gives TCXO FM sidebands -73..-93 dBc and LNA supply-AM sidebands -51 dBc, i.e. < 1e-4 dB of C/N0; u-blox specifies no VCC ripple limit at all (the only supply rule is <= 0.2 ohm series resistance, IM 4.1.1, met by the 8 mohm bead). With a 12-16 mA near-constant load there is nothing to excite ringing.

Test (verification only). AC-coupled scope at C11: a stable stage shows a 2.2 MHz sawtooth of 0.4-0.9 mV pp (ESR dominated, so it cannot be used to infer Ceff) and nothing in 50-500 kHz; the 2.2 MHz period also confirms S-CONF was read as VIN. DC at C6 3.33 +-0.05 V. 100 mA load step (33 ohm, MOSFET, ~1 kHz): settle in <= 2 ring cycles, and read dV/dt in the first 1-2 us for Ceff = I dt/dV (>= 40 uF). A biased-LCR check needs a bias-capable bridge; a handheld meter cannot apply 3.3 V.

### 3.6 The archive V2 buck carrier as a bench comparator

The archive `SAM10M8_18mm_HV_V2` board exists as a physical, never-reworked TPS62913 carrier (details in section 2.3). It has not, on the record, flown, so it contributes no field number; it is worth running in test step 1 beside the LDO and repo boards because it shares the regulator, the bead and the switch-node location with the repo board while differing in switching frequency (1 MHz vs 2.2 MHz), inductor type and distance, In2 net, via count and V_BCKP treatment. A healthy V2 beside a degraded repo board would localise any buck-related effect to those differences or to the reworked unit. Note for anyone reading the archive: the four numbered Save-As copies `SAM10M8_18mm_HV_V2 2/3/4/5.kicad_pcb` carry R5 = 2.43 k (a 1.20 V output, impossible for a board that ran the module); the un-numbered Jul-19 `.kicad_pcb` and Jul-20 `.kicad_sch` read 15.4 k and agree with the repo. Read values off the physical board before using it.
